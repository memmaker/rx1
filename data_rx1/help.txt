Welcome to rx1 - Rogue Variant I

Find the Amulet of Yendor on the 26th level of the dungeon and bring it back up.
Press = at any time for the exact key bindings of your current keymap.

Moving
======
w a s d        move north / west / south / east
W A S D        run in that direction
numpad 1-9     move in all eight directions (5: run in a direction)
.              wait a turn
> / <          go down / up the stairs you are standing on

Fighting
========
bump           walk into a monster to attack it
g              aim at a target
h              quick shot at the nearest enemy
v              throw an item
t              tactics menu

Items
=====
,              pick up
i              inventory: select an item to use, consume, equip,
               take off or drop it
e              use an item: read a scroll or document, zap a wand
I              list of items in view
O              show item overlay on the map

Looking around
==============
x              look: move the cursor over the map, select a monster
               to see its stats and lore (Tab switches tabs)
m              list of monsters in view
U              show monster overlay on the map
right click    open a monster's stats and lore

Information
===========
c              character sheet
l              message log
? or F2        this manual
= or F1        key bindings
Tab            command menu (every action with its key)
o              auto explore (any key stops it)

Screen
======
F3 / F4        darker / brighter
Ctrl+T         switch the colour theme
F7 / F8        save / load the game (hero, pack and every level seen)
Q              quit

Targeting
=========
While aiming or looking: move the cursor with wasd or the numpad,
Space switches between direction and free targeting,
Tab / Shift+Tab cycle through targets, Enter selects, Esc cancels.


Weapon patterns
===============
sword          a plain, honest attack on the monster you walk into
spear          also hits whatever stands directly behind your target
axe            hits every monster around you at once
club, mace     hit hard but slowly: every attack takes two turns
rapier         lunge: walk toward a monster two steps away across a
               free tile and you leap in and strike - never misses,
               triple damage
whip           walk toward a monster you can see up to 5 tiles away in a
               straight line with nothing in between and you lash it
               from where you stand
dagger         the assassin's blade: sneak attacks deal 5x instead of 3x

Sneak attacks
=============
A monster that is asleep, held, or has not noticed you yet is open to a
sneak attack. A sneak attack never misses and deals triple damage
(five times with a dagger). Every monster you strike notices you, unless
you are invisible.

Tips
====
- Sneak up on sleeping monsters with a dagger for a quick kill.
- Let a crowd surround you only when you hold an axe.
- Line monsters up in a corridor for the spear.
- Keep your distance with a whip; close it in a single step with a rapier.
- Swing a mace only when you can afford to give the monster a free turn.
