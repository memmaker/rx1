Welcome to rx1 - Rogue Variant I

Find the Amulet of Yendor on the 26th level of the dungeon and bring it back up.
Press = at any time for the exact key bindings of your current keymap.

Moving
======
w a s d        move north / west / south / east
W A S D        run in that direction
numpad 1-9     move in all eight directions (5: run in a direction)
.              wait a turn
Enter          take the stairs you are standing on (up or down)
> / <          go down / up the stairs you are standing on

Fighting
========
bump           walk into a monster to attack it
f              launch (fire your missile weapon)
g              aim at a target
h              quick shot at the nearest enemy
v              throw an item
t              tactics menu

Items
=====
,              pick up
i              inventory: select an item to quaff, read, eat, wear,
               wield, put on, remove, drop or zap it
e              use an item
r              read a scroll or a document you found
I              list of items in view
O              show item overlay on the map

Looking around
==============
x              look: move the cursor over the map, select a monster
               to see its stats and lore (Tab switches tabs)
m              list of monsters in view
U              show monster overlay on the map
right click    open a monster's stats and lore

Information
===========
c              character sheet
l              message log
? or F1         this help
=              key bindings

Screen
======
F2 / F3        darker / brighter
Ctrl+T         switch the colour theme
Q              quit

Targeting
=========
While aiming or looking: move the cursor with wasd or the numpad,
Space switches between direction and free targeting,
Tab / Shift+Tab cycle through targets, Enter selects, Esc cancels.
