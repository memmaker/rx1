[#f5e342]Origin[-]

The quagga was a South African plains zebra, striped only on its head and neck and plain brown behind. It was hunted to extinction in the 19th century; the last one died in the Artis zoo in Amsterdam in 1883. Like the emu and the kestrel, it has no source in Dungeons & Dragons, and it is a memorable pick for the awkward letter Q.

[#f5e342]Rogue Trivia[-]

The quagga is new in Rogue 5.x, where it took Q from the quasit, a demon invented by Dungeons & Dragons. In the IBM PC version the quagga even kept the quasit's numbers: its armor class of 2, its three weak attacks and almost the same experience. The rx1 quagga charges into battle, the way a horse would.

The description at the top comes from the Macintosh version of Rogue.
