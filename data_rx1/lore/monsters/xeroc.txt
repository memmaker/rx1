[#FFFFFF]Another creature rumored to have been brought by Yendor from another dimension. As you already know, they have the ability to disguise themselves as almost anything.[-]

[#f5e342]Origin[-]

The xeroc has no myth behind it. It is the mimic under a new name, and the name is said to be a joke: Xerox, the copier company, whose brand had become the everyday word for making a copy. A xeroc would then be a monster that copies things.

[#f5e342]Rogue Trivia[-]

When Rogue 5.x removed the monsters Dungeons & Dragons invented, the mimic was among them, but its trick was too good to lose. It came back at X as the xeroc, with the same disguise: it sits in a room looking like gold, a potion, a scroll or a staircase, until the hero touches it. The flavor line's "as you already know" assumes the player met it under its old name.

The description at the top comes from the Macintosh version of Rogue.
