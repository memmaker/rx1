[#f5e342]Origin[-]

Rattlesnakes are venomous pit vipers of the Americas, named for the rattle of hollow keratin segments at the tip of the tail that they shake as a warning. Their venom attacks blood and tissue, and a bite can leave a limb weak and swollen for weeks.

[#f5e342]Rogue Trivia[-]

The rattlesnake is new in Rogue 5.x, where it took R from the rust monster, whose armor-eating touch moved to the aquator. Its bite is poisonous: unless the hero saves against poison, "you feel a bite in your leg and now feel weaker", and a point of strength is lost.

The description at the top comes from the Macintosh version of Rogue.
