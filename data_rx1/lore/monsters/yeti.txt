[#f5e342]Origin[-]

The yeti is the "abominable snowman" of Himalayan legend, a huge ape-like creature said to roam the snowfields of Nepal and Tibet. Footprints photographed by the 1951 Everest expedition made it world famous. Dungeons & Dragons made it a predator of the ice:

    "Inhabiting only regions of icy cold, yeti are seldom encountered by warm-blooded mankind. Those who do have the misfortune to stumble upon these monsters seldom live to tell the tale, for yeti are very fond of human flesh."
    - Monster Manual (1977), p. 103

The same entry gives it armor class 6 and two claws for 1-6 each. Rogue kept all three numbers.

[#f5e342]Rogue Trivia[-]

The yeti holds Y in every version of Rogue. The rx1 yeti is fast, and like the Monster Manual's it can grab and squeeze its prey; its icy touch can also slow the hero down. True to its home in the snow, cold cannot harm it, while fire burns it for half again as much.

The description at the top comes from the Macintosh version of Rogue.
